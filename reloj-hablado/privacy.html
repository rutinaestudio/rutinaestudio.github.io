Privacy Policy — Talking Clock

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Talking Clock collects no personal data. No servers, no accounts, no tracking.

What data is collected

None. The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal information.

What is stored on your device

Your preferences (chosen interval and language) are stored on your iPhone only, using standard iOS storage. They never leave the device and are removed if you delete the app.

Internet connection

The app works offline. It sends and receives no data, except when iOS handles an in-app purchase, which is processed by Apple directly.

Advertising and analytics

None. No ads, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party tools.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through its payment system. I never receive or see your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date.
